# Future Scope & Planned Features

This document tracks future enhancements, feature ideas, and architectural extensions for ShopLedger.
*Note: Ideas logged here are for future iterations and should not be implemented in current initial sprints.*

---

## Planned Enhancements
- Multi-currency support and real-time exchange rate updates.
- Automated invoice generation and PDF exports.
- WhatsApp & SMS notifications for transaction alerts and customer receipts.
- Advanced analytics dashboard with sales forecasting models.
- Role-based access control (RBAC) with custom granular permissions.
- Offline-first support with background PWA sync.
